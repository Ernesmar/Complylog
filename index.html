Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started

Is ComplyLog Pro suitable for my industry? ▼
We support Food & Beverage, Cannabis, Pharmaceutical, Laboratory, Construction, Cosmetics, and Medical Devices. If your industry is regulated, we likely have templates for you.
Which countries do you support? ▼
USA, Spain, Germany, France, Italy, UK, Portugal, Mexico, Brazil, Argentina, Chile, Uruguay, Dominican Republic, and Colombia. We add new countries based on user demand.
Can I export audit-ready PDFs? ▼
Yes! Generate HACCP logs, compliance reports, and task summaries in PDF or CSV/Excel format with your company branding, signature, and regulatory references.
Is my data secure? ▼
Absolutely. We use Supabase (SOC 2 compliant) with Row Level Security, encrypted storage, and all API keys are server-side only. Your data never leaves your company's isolated environment.
Can I invite my team? ▼
Yes. Starter supports 1 user, Professional supports 5, and Enterprise is unlimited. Each member can have roles: Owner, Admin, Editor, or Viewer.
